Associate lawyer

Felipe Lanchares Pérez

Areas

  • Administrative, regulatory and environmental law

Contact

contacto@salinasycia.cl · contact form

Lawyer from Universidad Gabriela Mistral, with a Master's in Environmental Law from Universidad del Desarrollo. For more than thirteen years he worked at Chile's Ministry of National Assets: as a lawyer in the Department of Acquisition and Management of Assets from 2013, and as its head between 2021 and 2026, working on the acquisition, administration and management of state-owned property, environmental and real estate law, and non-conventional renewable energy.

At Salinas & Cía. he works as an associate lawyer on administrative and regulatory matters. Since 2021 he has taught Introduction to Law at the School of Public Administration of Universidad Mayor.

Education

  • Lawyer, Bachelor of Laws, Universidad Gabriela Mistral
  • Master's in Environmental Law, Universidad del Desarrollo (2017)
  • Postgraduate diploma in Environmental Law, Universidad del Desarrollo (2015)
