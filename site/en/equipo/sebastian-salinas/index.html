Lawyer and founding partner of Salinas & Cía. Abogados (2011). Admitted to the bar by the Supreme Court of Chile on 26 August 2011. He advises companies, entrepreneurs, business families and individuals on business law, corporate governance, real estate law and civil litigation.

He is also co-founder and CEO of Dunora (LEXINNOVA SpA), a legal AI platform for law firms, and a licensed Public Auctioneer (Registry No. 1759, since 2017).

Education

  • Bachelor of Laws (Licenciado en Ciencias Jurídicas y Sociales), Universidad Gabriela Mistral
  • Master's in Business Law, Universidad de los Andes
  • Executive MBA (Magíster en Dirección General de Empresas), Universidad Gabriela Mistral
  • Master's in General Management, Universitat de Lleida (Spain)
  • Diploma in Corporate Real Estate Law, Universidad de los Andes
  • Member of the Chilean Bar Association (Colegio de Abogados de Chile)
