How we work

Understand before deciding

We work with a simple idea: clients should understand their legal situation and options before deciding.

One responsible lawyer
Each matter has a lawyer in charge from start to finish, who is your direct point of contact.
Written proposal
Before starting, a clear assessment and a written proposal of work and fees.
Associate specialists
When the matter requires it, we bring in associate lawyers with specific experience, coordinated by the firm.
Professional confidentiality
All the information you give us is protected by professional secrecy.

Mission

Since 2011 we have advised individuals, families, business owners and companies with a comprehensive view of their affairs, from the personal to the corporate. We build a close relationship of trust with each client, with one lawyer responsible from start to finish who combines legal judgment with business sense, so that clients decide well informed and on time.

Vision

To be the trusted law firm of the families and companies who rely on us: the place where they resolve their legal, personal and business matters, in a relationship that lasts over time.
