The Firm

The Firm

Salinas & Cía. Abogados was founded in 2011 by Sebastián Salinas Fuenzalida to provide efficient and comprehensive legal counsel to companies, entrepreneurs, business families and individuals.

Mission

Since 2011 we have advised individuals, families, business owners and companies with a comprehensive view of their affairs, from the personal to the corporate. We build a close relationship of trust with each client, with one lawyer responsible from start to finish who combines legal judgment with business sense, so that clients decide well informed and on time.

Vision

To be the trusted law firm of the families and companies who rely on us: the place where they resolve their legal, personal and business matters, in a relationship that lasts over time.

How we work

We believe good legal counsel shows in the client's decisions: understanding the risks, knowing the options and acting on time. That is why we work with:

  • One responsible lawyer for each matter, from start to finish.
  • Written proposals, with clear scope and fees before starting.
  • Associate specialists when the matter requires it, coordinated by the firm.
  • Professional confidentiality over all the information you give us.
