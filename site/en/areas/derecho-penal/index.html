Practice area

Criminal law

We advise and represent individuals and companies in criminal proceedings: as defendants, exercising their right to a defence from the first summons, and as victims, pursuing those who harmed them. We also help companies prevent criminal risks.

Services

  • Defence of persons under investigation, from the first statement before the Public Prosecutor's Office or the police.
  • Private criminal complaints (querellas) on behalf of victims.
  • Economic and property crimes: fraud, misappropriation and forgery.
  • Detention control, formal charging and precautionary measure hearings.
  • Criminal liability of legal entities and crime prevention models.
  • Appeals before the Courts of Appeal and the Supreme Court.

Frequently asked questions

I have been summoned by the Public Prosecutor's Office. Should I go with a lawyer?+

You have the right to be assisted by a lawyer from the very first step. It is advisable to consult before giving a statement, to understand your procedural situation and your rights.

What is a querella?+

It is the action through which the victim takes an active part in criminal proceedings: requesting investigative steps, taking part in hearings and asking for the person responsible to be convicted.

Can a company be criminally liable?+

Yes. Chilean law establishes the criminal liability of legal entities for certain offences. An adequate crime prevention model is the main tool to reduce that risk.
