Practice area

Contracts, negotiation and legal risk management

We draft, review and negotiate contracts for your business or personal life, and help you spot legal risks before they turn into disputes.

Services

  • Drafting and review of commercial and civil contracts.
  • Support in negotiations.
  • Share and asset purchase agreements.
  • Legal audit of existing contracts.
  • Legal risk assessment for companies.

Frequently asked questions

Is it worth having a lawyer review a contract before signing?+

Yes, especially if it is long-term, involves significant amounts or has penalty or termination clauses. A prior review usually costs far less than a later dispute.

Can you support me in a negotiation?+

Yes. We can prepare the negotiation with you, take part in meetings or review each version of the agreement.

What is a legal audit?+

It is an orderly review of a company's contracts, permits and documents to detect risks and propose how to correct them.
