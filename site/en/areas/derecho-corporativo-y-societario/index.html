Practice area

Corporate and company law

We advise companies and entrepreneurs throughout the legal life of their companies, from incorporation to restructuring or winding up. Our goal is for the corporate structure to support the business, not to become a problem.

Services

  • Incorporation of companies (SpA, limited liability companies, corporations) and choice of the right legal form.
  • Amendments to bylaws, capital increases and admission or exit of partners.
  • Shareholder and partner agreements.
  • Shareholder meetings, minutes and up-to-date corporate records.
  • Restructurings, mergers, spin-offs and dissolution of companies.
  • Incorporation of non-profit corporations and foundations.

Frequently asked questions

What type of company should I use to start a business?+

It depends on the number of partners, how you want to share control and profits, and your growth plans. The SpA is flexible and widely used, but it is not always the best option. We review it with you before incorporating.

What is a shareholder agreement for?+

To agree in writing on what the bylaws do not cover: how certain decisions are made, what happens if a partner wants to sell or leave, and how disagreements are resolved. It prevents conflicts that are costly to settle later.

My company has not updated its corporate records for years. Is that a problem?+

It can be when selling, seeking financing or facing a dispute between partners. We review your corporate situation and propose how to bring it up to date.
