Practice area

Real estate law

We advise on the purchase, sale, lease and regularisation of property, and on complex situations such as attachments and judicial auctions.

Services

  • Title reviews and property purchases and sales.
  • Lease agreements, evictions and precarious-occupancy lawsuits.
  • Inheritance and probate of real estate.
  • Defence in foreclosure auctions and negotiation with creditors.
  • Claims against the Real Estate Registrar (Conservador de Bienes Raíces).
  • Updating and amending condominium regulations under Law 21,442 on Condominium Ownership.
  • Real estate projects and investment.

Frequently asked questions

What is a title review and why is it done?+

It is the legal review of a property's history to confirm that the seller is the owner and that there are no liens, prohibitions or other issues affecting the purchase.

My property has been attached. What can I do?+

There are options depending on the stage of the lawsuit: negotiating with the creditor, selling the property before the auction or raising the relevant defences. The sooner the case is reviewed, the more options there are.

What can I do if the Registrar refuses to record my deed?+

The law allows that refusal to be challenged in court. We review the Registrar's reasons and, where appropriate, file the claim.

Does my building's or condominium's regulation need updating?+

Law 21,442 changed the rules on condominium ownership, and many older regulations are now outdated. We review the current regulation, propose its update and support the approval process at the owners' meeting through to its registration.
