Practice area

Inheritance and estate planning

We support families throughout an inheritance, from probate (posesión efectiva) to the partition of the assets. We also help plan succession during one's lifetime, to organise assets and prevent disputes among heirs.

Services

  • Probate (posesión efectiva), with or without a will.
  • Registration of inherited property with the Real Estate Registrar.
  • Partition of estates, by agreement or through proceedings.
  • Drafting of wills.
  • Estate planning for families and business owners.
  • Sale of inherited property.

Frequently asked questions

What is the posesión efectiva?+

It is the procedure that recognises who the heirs of a deceased person are and allows them to deal with the estate's assets. Without a will it is processed before the Civil Registry; with a will, before a court.

Can we sell an inherited property?+

Yes, but first the posesión efectiva and the inheritance registrations must be completed. If there are several heirs, all must take part in the sale or the estate must be partitioned.

What happens if the heirs cannot agree?+

A partition can be requested before an arbitrator, who decides how to divide the assets. Before that, we always seek an agreement.
