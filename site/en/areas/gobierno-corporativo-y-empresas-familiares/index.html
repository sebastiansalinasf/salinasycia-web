Practice area

Corporate governance and family businesses

We help companies and business families organise how decisions are made: who decides what, how the board works and how the handover to the next generation is prepared.

Services

  • Family protocols and agreements between family shareholders.
  • Design and operation of boards of directors and committees.
  • Succession planning in ownership and management.
  • Ongoing legal counsel to boards of directors.
  • Structures to separate family and business assets.

Frequently asked questions

What is a family protocol?+

It is an agreement among the members of a business family that governs their relationship with the company: who may work in it, how directors are chosen and what happens to the shares on a sale or an inheritance.

When should business succession be planned?+

While the founder is active and can take part. Planning ahead allows ownership and management to be organised without pressure and reduces the risk of family disputes.

Does a medium-sized company need a board?+

It is not always mandatory, but a well-designed board helps make better decisions and separate family matters from business matters.
